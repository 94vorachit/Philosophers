if (philo->sim->philo_count == 1)

### Why this is the next step
This is the important special-case rule from the project:
- a single philosopher cannot eat
- they take one fork and then die after `time_to_die`
- without this case, the logic deadlocks or misbehaves

This is still a very focused fix and it belongs right before final validation and cleanup polishing.
